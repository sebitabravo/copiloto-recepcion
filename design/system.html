Copiloto Recepción tokens

Two systems live here: the public landing (landing/demo.css) and the reception panel (copiloto/src/copiloto/static/theme.css). The panel replicates the landing tokens, but several values have drifted — conflicts are rendered side by side below. Everything on this page is a value declared in one of those two files.

Typography

Families: Instrument Serif (display), Inter (UI), and a mono stack (landing only). Webfonts are declared in the sources but not loaded on this artboard — the system fallbacks (Georgia / system-ui / Menlo) render.

Families

Display — "Instrument Serif", "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Georgia, serif both

UI — Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif landing --font-ui panel --font-body (no ui-sans-serif)

Mono — ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, "SF Mono", Menlo, monospace landing only

Weights used: 400 (headings, body, display numerals) · 500 · 600 · 650 (brand, eyebrow) · 700. Inter @font-face: 400–700 / optional (landing) vs 100–900 / swap (panel).

Shadows & motion

Landing declares a three-step scale; the panel declares a single --shadow. Both declare dark-mode overrides (not shown here). Easing differs: landing uses cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .3, 1), the panel tokens --ease: cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1).

Motion

landing cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .3, 1) on .2–.7s transitions; keyframes aurora 18s, ticker 40s linear, message-in .28s, typing 1s, stack-float 12s; reveal stagger .08/.16/.24s. Reduced motion: all animations and transitions disabled.

panel --ease: cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1); keyframes rise .42s, pop .5s (stagger var(--i) × 60ms), pulse-red, shimmer 1.4s. Reduced motion: durations .001ms.
