Copiloto Recepción tokens

Two systems live here: the public landing (landing/demo.css) and the reception panel (copiloto/src/copiloto/static/theme.css). The panel replicates the landing tokens, but several values have drifted — conflicts are rendered side by side below. Product values below come only from those two files. Board layout, swatch geometry and local CSS aliases are presentation aids, not project tokens. No systems have been merged.

Colors

Light page chrome matches; dark accents differ. Landing declares 27 color tokens and panel 15. WhatsApp tokens belong only to landing. Sources: landing/demo.css and copiloto/src/copiloto/static/theme.css.

Typography

Sources declare Instrument Serif (display), Inter (UI), and mono (landing only): 3 landing stacks, 2 panel stacks. This board applies only their system-font fallbacks; actual fallback selection depends on the OS. No fonts or other assets are fetched.

Families

Display — "Instrument Serif", "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Georgia, serif both

UI — Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif landing --font-ui panel --font-body (no ui-sans-serif)

Mono — ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, "SF Mono", Menlo, monospace landing only

Weights used: 400 (headings, body, display numerals) · 500 · 600 · 650 (brand, eyebrow) · 700. Inter @font-face: 400–700 (landing) vs 100–900 (panel). All source faces now use font-display: swap.

Motion

landing cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .3, 1) on .25–.7s transitions; keyframes aurora 18s, ticker 40s linear, message-in .28s (.32s on .event-card), typing 1s, stack-float 12s; reveal stagger .08/.16/.24s. Reduced motion: all animations and transitions disabled.

panel --ease: cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1); keyframes rise .42s, pop .5s (stagger var(--i) × 60ms), pulse-red, shimmer 1.4s. Reduced motion: durations .001ms.
